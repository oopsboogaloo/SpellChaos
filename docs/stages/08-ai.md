## Implementation interpretation

Wizard decisions take only `projectView` output, an explicit AI seed and bounded
policy options. Own planning capabilities/status are projected explicitly; enemy
HP/mana/orders, illusion truth/knowledge, hidden occupants and source provenance
are excluded. A synthetic knowledge state may validate candidate orders using
normal engine rules, but it contains only observed data and conservative guesses.
All players' views are frozen before any AI draft is submitted. Difficulty changes
search budget/scoring only, never stats, observations or rules.

AI decisions use the AI random stream only. A system-only planning command records
its explicit stream transition; draft/lock commands use the ordinary player API.
The coordinator publishes each planning batch atomically. If an uncertain order
is rejected by authoritative validation, submit one fixed empty-action fallback
without retrying alternatives or feeding the rejection reason into the policy.
Diagnostic fallback counts remain harness information, never policy input.

Unbound units use their own perception, not a summoner's or another unit's view.
At StartResolution, snapshot the nearest perceived living different-species target;
equal-distance identity ties use the AI stream, paths use canonical deterministic
search. No legal progress gives Total Defence. A restrained unit may Escape and a
spent siege weapon may Reload; attacks, movement, heights, Subdued/Double Action,
range and sight use ordinary mechanics. Binding/control loss does not transfer
old orders; newly unbound units wait until the next planning boundary.

Unbound plans and their actor identities are stored before resolution. Historical
mid-resolution snapshots receive an empty autonomous-actor set, preserving their
committed behavior; later turns use the explicitly migrated current rules. Freeze
schema-26 validators before introducing schema 27 and never rewrite fixtures.

The portable match harness advances planning batches, StartResolution and each
engine atomic step. It records policy/configuration versions, explicit operation
limits and counters, outcomes, snapshots and private failure diagnostics. Wins,
draws, harness timeouts and errors are distinct; resource limits never manufacture
engine victory or draws. Headless scenarios are not a substitute for the Stage 9
playable interface or the Stage 10 acceptance/performance/soak suite.

## Implementation evidence

- `packages/ai/src/policy.ts`: view-only policy, deterministic bounded scoring,
  difficulty budgets, visible threat/friendly-fire checks, attack/power/reload/
  escape/mount decisions and candidates for all twelve spell families and five
  summon families. It accepts no authoritative state or validation callback.
- `knowledge.ts`: explicit own readiness/budget/capabilities, public apparent
  traits and corpse targeting metadata build a conservative synthetic world.
  Unknown enemy binding strength/hidden occupancy cannot become validation
  oracles. Secret illusion provenance and other observers' knowledge are excluded.
- `coordinator.ts`: frozen observations for all players, explicit AI-stream command,
  normal player orders/locks, no alternative retries after authoritative rejection.
- `engine/src/unbound.ts`: per-observer visibility, nearest different apparent
  family/level species (including living/reanimated forms), seeded target ties,
  ordinary attack resolution, bounded detours, flight, Escape/Reload and known
  Early waking. Newly unbound units wait for next StartResolution.
- `engine/src/navigation.ts`: bounded canonical A*, legal transit/resting prefixes,
  height transitions, wall/weed costs and exact retained-prefix movement cost.
- `session.ts`: JSON-safe versioned coordinator/counters/configuration plus native
  engine snapshots. Limits produce timeouts, native terminal results produce
  wins/draws, unexpected errors preserve the last valid private failure state.
- Six permanent session fixtures cover planning, ready, unbound movement,
  unbound attack, an explicit resource limit and a complete default-book match.
  Node and Chromium compare every published boundary fingerprint, native events,
  final private session and permitted player views after fresh restores.

Schema 27 / rules `autonomy-0.1` / content `gryphon-shepherd-0.1` introduces
`autonomous` actor identities and system-only `AdvanceAiStream`. Frozen schema-26
state/combat/availability validators and `migrateRosterSnapshot` preserve old
orders, RNG, abilities and pending work. Historical fixtures are unchanged.

## Reproduction and smoke report

Build, then run:

```
node tools/run-ai.mjs --players 2,4,8 --seeds 123,456 --max-turns 60 --output /tmp/ai-report.json
node tools/run-ai.mjs --players 3,5,6,7 --seeds 123 --max-turns 60 --output /tmp/ai-extra-report.json
```

`docs/reports/stage-08-smoke.json` records the final policy/configuration versions,
seeds, work/step counts, fallback counts and fingerprints. Ten matches cover every
player count from two through eight. Eight reached engine victory; 2-player
seed 456 and 3-player seed 123 reached the 60-turn harness limit.
There were no draws or failures. The timeout remains a live engine state, not an
invented draw. This is a reproducible smoke sample, not balance or soak evidence.

Difficulty changes bounded search/jitter only; rules/stats and observations are
identical. The limited heuristic policy does not promise every seed terminates
under a fixed turn cap. The report separately records fixed-fallback proposals
where hidden binding/placement facts prevent authoritative acceptance. Rejection
reasons are never provided to the policy or used to choose an alternate candidate.

Existing nine CLI scenarios remain, and `run-ai.mjs` adds a tenth; CI executes the
AI smoke alongside typechecking, unit/build and Chromium continuation checks.
Stage 9's finished playable UI and Stage 10 acceptance/performance/soak work remain
separate. No deployment is introduced.

Stage 8 pull request; this checkpoint does not authorise merging it.

PR #40 was merged while this work was in progress. Its Stage 1–7 review record
and owner decisions are incorporated. Existing engine findings remain tracked

planning-validator leaks; they do not repair the general human command API.
